# Swiggy Sales & Performance Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze and uncover actionable insights from historical Swiggy food delivery metrics, processing over **197K orders** and **₹53M+** in revenue.
## 🔑 Key KPIs & Insights Tracked
* **Financial Performance:** Analyzed ₹53,012,506 in total sales with an Average Order Value (AOV) of ₹268.51.
* **Order Volumes:** Monitored 197,430 total orders against a vast customer response footprint (5.5M+ rating counts).
* **Geographical Distribution:** Identified Bengaluru as the highest revenue-generating city (₹5.4M+), followed closely by Lucknow and Mumbai.
* **Vendor Ecosystem:** Tracked market dominance among the top 10 restaurant partners, highlighting massive volume blocks for KFC, McDonald's, and Domino's Pizza.
* **Consumer Preference:** Visualized sales splits by food type, showing a strong consumer preference for Non-Veg items (₹33.8M) compared to Veg items (₹19.1M).

## 🛠️ Data Engineering & Visualizations Built
* **Temporal Tracking:** Line charts mapping performance fluctuations across months (Jan–Aug) and days of the week.
* **Dynamic Slicers:** Enabled granular filtering by Month, Food Type, Category, and State boundaries.
* **Advanced Visuals:** Integrated Treemaps for brand positioning, Donut charts for demographic splits, and filled regional shape maps.
